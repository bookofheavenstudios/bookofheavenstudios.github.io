Historical back ground 2 Samuel

2 Samuel Historical Background 01-09-2015 (same as 1st Samuel)

These Books are set in the time that the Nation of Israel is transitioning from a Theocracy to a Kingdom. The land around them consisted of city/states. Towns were walled communit then ies behind which the residents could resist invaders. Although the farmers went out to cultivate their fields, and shepherds went out to tend their flocks, they lived in towns for safety. At this time Israel is one continuous country from about as far north as Damascus and as far south as the gulf of Aquaba. Under Solomon, David’s son it grew even more. Under the reign of Rehoboam, the grandson of David through Solomon, in about 800 a.d. , the nation was divided. The Northern Kingdom called Israel and the Southern Kingdom called Judah. The South consisted of the tribes of Judah, Levi and Benjamin. Israel consisted of the other ten tribes. They were Reuben, Simeon, Zebulon, Issachar, Dan, Gad, Asher, Naphtali, and Joseph’s two sons, Manessa and Ephraim Theis division will not be healed until the 1000 year reign, although at this time it is not evident in modern Israel.

So in David’s time we see a unified Israel. It’s division is the result of Solomon and Rehoboam’s sin. The actual split comes 100 years after David.

Introduction 2 Samuel

This book is a continuation of 1st Samuel. The theme of 1st Samuel is continued and a kingdom is the form of government in view. We have watched David’s early years as God brings him to be the Ruler of Judah. Ahead of us we will see him consolidate his kingdom to include Israel as well. We must read the first two chapters of First Kings in order to follow David to his death. He died after making sure that Solomon was firmly on the throne of Israel.

David wrote many psalms. We will be readng them at the appropriate times.

We see in this book David’s national victories (Chapters 1-10) followed by his personal defeats in Chapters 11-24. David confessed his sins, and submitted to God’s discipline and spent the closing years preparing the materials for a Temple for God.. The book would be a great expostion of the following two verses.

Proverbs 14:34 (NASB)

34Righteousness exalts a nation, But sin is a disgrace to any people.

Proverbs 28:13 (NASB)

13He who conceals his transgressions will not prosper, But he who confesses and forsakes them will find compassion.

God’s chosen form of government is a kingdom ruled by a king. Yet to change the form of our government today would not solve our problems. The problem is people. What ever form that government takes it will fail because of the people. Romans 3:10 as it is written, "THERE IS NONE RIGHTEOUS, NOT EVEN ONE; 11 THERE IS NONE WHO UNDERSTANDS, THERE IS NONE WHO SEEKS FOR GOD; 12 ALL HAVE TURNED ASIDE, TOGETHER THEY HAVE BECOME USELESS; THERE IS NONE WHO DOES GOOD, THERE IS NOT EVEN ONE." 13 "THEIR THROAT IS AN OPEN GRAVE, WITH THEIR TONGUES THEY KEEP DECEIVING," "THE POISON OF ASPS IS UNDER THEIR LIPS"; 14 "WHOSE MOUTH IS FULL OF CURSING AND BITTERNESS"; 15 "THEIR FEET ARE SWIFT TO SHED BLOOD, 16 DESTRUCTION AND MISERY ARE IN THEIR PATHS, 17 AND THE PATH OF PEACE HAVE THEY NOT KNOWN." 18 "THERE IS NO FEAR OF GOD BEFORE THEIR EYES."

However a kingdom is God’s ideal and some day Jesus will rule the Earth as its King. What we need is a King who rules in righteousness. A King who will depend on God and a King who obeys God.
